## Rules evidence

Consulted 28 October 2026. MCC's [official laws index](https://www.lords.org/mcc/about-the-laws-of-cricket)
links the [2017 Code, fourth edition (2026)](https://www.lords.org/getattachment/8c96a135-beff-4a46-b5c6-2a02a7245386/Laws-of-Cricket-2017-Code-4th-Edition-(2026).pdf),
effective 1 October 2026, and its [explanation of changes](https://www.lords.org/getattachment/39ccb9ea-b159-443e-9f1f-0358eb46d01e/Changes-to-Laws-for-2026-edition-explained.pdf).

- 21.17: on a no-ball only hit ball twice, obstructing the field or run out can dismiss a batter.
- 22.9: on a wide, hit wicket, obstructing the field, run out or stumped are possible.
- 18.12.1: crossing on the unfinished run affects the surviving batter's end on a run out.
- 25.4.2–4: retired hurt/unavoidable cause permits return at a wicket or another retirement;
  other retirement can require opposing captain consent. The app records operator decisions;
  it cannot establish that consent or umpire approval occurred.
- 16.6: winning extras/completed runs precede later events; genuine boundary allowances differ.
- 16.5.1: equal scores with all innings complete are tied. The fix addresses final all-out;
  incomplete/early-closed games still need an explicit result workflow.
- 38.3: if a non-striker run-out occurs without delivery, the ball does not count in the over.

Free hits and replacement of extra balls are existing configurable competition choices.
This batch preserves those settings. It does not calculate DLS, infer umpire decisions,
or declare a complete competition ruleset.

### Completed behavior changes

- Wide-ball stumping/hit wicket records the wide penalty and bowler wicket,
  while standard rules leave the delivery uncounted and the batter's balls faced
  unchanged. Local settings can still count wides in the over.
- A shared dismissal/outcome/free-hit check rejects impossible combinations with
  an actionable explanation. Non-ball retirement uses the existing player workflow.
- Wicket entry exposes the delivery outcome for all wickets. Run-outs and
  umpire-confirmed obstruction can retain completed runs/crossing; catch-preventing
  obstruction still requires the operator to enter disallowed runs/original ends
  correctly. No umpire decision is inferred by the app.
- A winning wide takes precedence over a later stumping. No-ball/bat/bye/leg-bye
  run-out combinations, sixth-ball selection and history replay remain covered.
- Returning eligible retired batters clears retired status and dismissal category;
  previous retirement checkpoints remain reversible and survive reload.
- Open entry forms detect changed non-striker, innings, deliveries, conditions,
  revision and finalisation, including changes during asynchronous confirmation.
- Finalised matches reject delivery replay and direct the operator to the existing
  scorecard correction workflow, which recalculates the stored result.
- Equal aggregate scores after final all-out in a four-innings game display tied.
- Exhausted/empty training Skip and duplicate finish callbacks cannot move the
  active position past timers added later or consume an unfinished replacement.
- Existing styles and motion remain in use; Cancel is visible beside Confirm,
  run-crossing choices are reachable and the preview identifies the next striker.

### Ranked next batch / decisions

1. **Undelivered non-striker run-out / non-ball events:** a distinct reversible
   event/checkpoint is needed under MCC 38.3. Do not fake a legal dot-ball wicket.
2. **Explicit match dispositions:** no result, abandonment, conceded/awarded result,
   draw/time expiry and revised-target provenance. Confirm the competition's
   minimum-overs and tie-break rules; early closure alone must not choose them.
3. **Eligibility and replacement workflows:** unavailable players, no eligible
   incoming batter, retired out returning with consent, junior retirement limits,
   substitute/concussion/replacement rules. Confirm supported competitions first.
4. **Rules profiles:** MCC 2026 versus the competition's adopted edition, free-hit
   rules, counted extras and current declaration restrictions. Preserve existing
   settings until an explicit profile is chosen; no automatic DLS calculation.
5. **Correction depth:** cross-innings reversal with explicit confirmation,
   milestone/presentation queue cancellation after undo, five-run penalty events,
   disallowed/short runs and boundary-versus-completed-running entry distinctions.
6. **Remote/session recovery:** old commands after reconnect/cache eviction or
   application restart, malformed saves, partial imports and actionable failures.
7. **Platform warning work and native acceptance:** target nullable adapter paths
   and dynamic properties, then real Mac window/focus/Retina/modal/popup checks.
   Test the reported AFL mouse/keyboard freeze on a real Mac for at least ten minutes.

These remaining areas are not represented as implemented or exhaustively tested.
The executor is Linux: it cannot establish actual Cocoa focus, multiple-display,
fullscreen, permission, input or long-running AFL behavior. No Mac CI was dispatched
for this batch. Existing Mac-runner headless results concern the earlier preview.
