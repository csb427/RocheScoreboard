# Roche Scoreboard 5.0

In development. No 5.0 release has been published.

## New

- Full cricket scoring for limited-overs and multi-innings matches, with toss, lineups, extras and wickets.
- Editable cricket scorecards and delivery history, innings controls and revised overs or chase targets.
- Cricket batting and bowling cards, scoreworm, Manhattan and automatic match overlays.
- Four, six, wicket, half-century and century celebrations.
- Redesigned control panel across sport selection, setup, live scoring, settings and match prompts.
- Liquid-glass navigation, animated controls, team theming and layouts that adapt to smaller windows.
- Cricket club search includes Victorian Premier and VSDCA teams, club logos and saved presets.

## Fixed

- Safer match recovery, more stable navigation and consistent shared output sizing.
